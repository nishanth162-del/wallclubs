Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Wallclubs—here’s how we keep our community thriving!

1. Content Submission

All articles must be original, tech-focused, and valuable to our community. Submissions are reviewed by our admins and may be approved or rejected based on quality, relevance, and adherence to guidelines.

2. Approval Process

Submitted content starts as "pending." If approved, it goes live; if rejected, you’ll receive an email with the reason. We aim for fair, constructive feedback to help you improve.

3. Rewards & Points

Points may be awarded for approved contributions—amounts and rules can change. We reserve the right to revert points if errors, bugs, or abuse occur, ensuring fairness.

4. Admin Authority

Wallclubs admins can modify, approve, reject, or remove content and adjust user points to maintain quality and integrity. Changes will be communicated clearly.

5. User Responsibility

You’re responsible for your content—keep it respectful, original, and tech-relevant. Violations may lead to rejection or account review.
